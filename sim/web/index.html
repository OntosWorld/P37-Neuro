How will P37 be connected?

After model export and native/browser parity tests, a verified P37 ONNX policy can be attached to the same MuJoCo scene through mjswan. Only then will task or velocity control be offered.

Research status: These are real robot simulation models with physics-based dynamics. They are virtual models, not remotely controlled physical robots. Model-only movement and contact responses do not constitute evidence of trained P37 intelligence. No fabricated results or scripted walking are shown.
