What does this preview validate?

Real physics, not P37 inference — yet. These links launch public models in MuJoCo Live, hosted by Google DeepMind. A model standing, falling or moving under gravity is not evidence of a P37 trained policy. No benchmark scores or claimed P37 improvements are shown.

Next release gate

The first genuine P37 G1 demonstration requires a policy checkpoint trained for the G1 observation/action contract, native-vs-browser inference parity, deterministic disturbance trials, actuator safety enforcement, and a report with held-out seeds.
